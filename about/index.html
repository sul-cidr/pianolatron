About the Pianolatron

The Pianolatron is the outcome of a large-scale collaborative partnership between faculty investigators, students and staff affiliated with the Stanford University Department of Music and the Stanford University Libraries. The technical implementation of the Pianolatron app, led by the developer team of the Stanford Center for Interdisciplinary Digital Research (CIDR), now part of Research Data Services in the Libraries, benefited greatly from contributions by other project participants, the worldwide network of player piano scholars and enthusiasts, and the open-source software community.

Source Code

The source code for the site is freely available under the MIT License at https://github.com/sul-cidr/pianolatron

The Pianolatron makes significant use of modules from the following open-source software projects:

  • @tonejs/piano, principal developer: Yotam Mann
  • MidiPlayerJS, principal developer: Garret Grimm
  • OpenSeadragon, principal developer: Ian Gilman
  • node-interval-tree, principal developer: Marko Žarković
  • pkspell: Francesco Foscarin, Nicolas Audebert, and Raphaël Fournier S’niehotta, “PKSpell: Data-Driven Pitch Spelling and Key Signature Estimation,” in Proceedings of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR), 2021.
